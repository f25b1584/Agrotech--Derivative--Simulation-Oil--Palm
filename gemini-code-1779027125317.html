Focus: Calculus $f'(x)$ in Malaysian Agriculture

🌱 The Bioscience Scenario

In Malaysian agrotechnology, maximizing early vegetative growth of oil palm (Elaeis guineensis) is vital for minimizing the immature phase before commercial harvesting.

This interactive simulator models the Canopy Projection Area (CPA) of a high-yield tenera oil palm clone during its first 60 months post-field planting, using a biological Logistic Growth function.

Data & Biological Reference Framework:

Parameters modeled based on growth dynamics reported by the Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) in their physiological evaluation benchmarks ("MPOB Physiological Standards for Elite Cloning Material").

🧮 Mathematical Modeling

The total growth follows the function $f(x)$, whereas the actual instantaneous rate of expansion at any month is captured by the first derivative $f'(x)$.

Total Canopy Area Function:
$$f(x) = \frac{35}{1 + e^{-0.12(x - 30)}}$$

Where $f(x)$ is the canopy area in $\text{m}^2$, and $x$ is the time in months.

Instantaneous Growth Rate Function:
$$f'(x) = \frac{0.12 \cdot 35 \cdot e^{-0.12(x - 30)}}{(1 + e^{-0.12(x - 30)})^2}$$

Calculates the precise rate of area expansion in $\text{m}^2/\text{month}$.
